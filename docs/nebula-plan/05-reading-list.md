# Reading list

Start with the five in "Read first"; the rest are for when a specific step needs them. Most are free on arXiv.

## Read first

| Paper | Why |
| --- | --- |
| [Mapelli & Trani 2016](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1510.05259), circumnuclear ring from a disrupted cloud | The template for Act 1: setup, turbulence, timescales, what forms |
| [Genzel, Eisenhauer & Gillessen 2010](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.0064), Galactic Centre review | What's really around Sgr A\*: minispiral, ring, ionizing stars |
| [Bonnell & Rice 2008](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.2723), star formation around SMBHs | A turbulent cloud's structure survives disruption; their k⁻⁴ setup |
| [Price et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.09381v2), Eddington envelopes | Phantom TDE with a year of evolution, with animations; the closest published cousin of your close-up |
| [Genetti et al., Orion Nebula visualization (AMNH/SDSC)](https://www.cs.uaf.edu/~genetti/Research/Papers/EG00/Orion.html) | How a planetarium built a fly-through nebula: skins, dark cores, nested volumes |

## Galactic Centre gas

| Paper | Why |

| [Ballone, Mapelli & Trani 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.00549v1) | Grid simulation linking the circumnuclear disc to a nearby cloud |

| [Schartmann et al. 2012](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1203.6356) | G2 hydrodynamics: tidal stretching, instabilities |
| [Guillochon et al. 2014](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.2990) | Cooling-driven clumps in a stream; recombination glow fades past ~10⁵ K |
| [JWST Sagittarius C, 2025](https://research.chalmers.se/en/publication/545962) | Magnetically shaped ionized filaments near the Galactic Centre |
