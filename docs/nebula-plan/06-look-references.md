# Look references

Study these before each look-dev round. Pause on single frames and note three things: where the brightest gas sits relative to the dark gas, how many depth layers you can count, and what moves fastest on screen.

## Real images (what nebulae actually look like)

| Reference | Look at | Steal this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hubble's Lagoon Nebula (search "Hubble Lagoon Nebula") | Pink hydrogen glow, black dust lanes, bright rims on dark clouds | Rims on dark shapes; contrast between glow and lanes |
| [M17 in natural colour (Clark)](https://clarkvision.com/galleries/gallery.astrophoto-1/web/m17-500mm-09-04-2021-acr-IMG_0993-003-av10-c2-1x-rs2,2-e.67s.html) | Pink-magenta core turning redder at the edges, teal where oxygen is bright, burnt-orange dust around | True-colour palette and dust reddening at edges |
| [Orion Trapezium in natural colour (Clark)](https://clarkvision.com/articles/astrophotography.m42-trapezium.true.color) | Teal centre near the hottest stars | Where teal belongs: only near hot stars |
| [Webb: Sagittarius C](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/sagittarius-c-nircam-image/) | Needle-like ionized filaments, a dark cloud in silhouette, ~500,000 stars | Galactic Centre gas is stringy; stars at every depth. Its colours are infrared shifted into visible, so copy shapes, not colours. |
| [MeerKAT + Webb: Milky Way centre](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/milky-way-center-meerkat-and-webb-labeled) | Huge vertical filaments around Sgr A\* | The sense of scale for an opening shot |

## Flythroughs (how pros make 2D nebulae feel 3D)

| Reference | Look at | Steal this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [STScI/Caltech Orion flythrough](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30957) | A valley of gas, the front "veil", stars passing by | Separate layers; slow, steady camera |
| [Mystic Mountain, Carina](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30683) | Pillars split into depth layers | Pillars: dark core, glowing edge, jets at the tips |
| [Pillars of Creation, Hubble + Webb](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/hubble/new-hubble-webb-pillars-of-creation-visualization) | Glowing pillar edges with wisps peeling off | Wisps leaving a dense edge in one direction, as if blown |
| [Cosmos (BUF)](https://buf.com/films/cosmos-a-spacetime-odyssey) | Nebulae that look frozen mid-motion; huge zooms | The dive between scales |
| Interstellar, Gargantua scenes | Lensed backgrounds and disc with no flicker | Smoothness: no sparkle in fine detail |
